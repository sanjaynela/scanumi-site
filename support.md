# Scanumi Support

Need a hand? Email [scanumi@outlook.com](mailto:scanumi@outlook.com) with your device model, iOS/iPadOS version, and a short description of what happened. Please do not attach a sensitive scan.

## Quick help

### The camera does not open

Open **Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera** and allow camera access for Scanumi. The document camera requires a supported physical iPhone or iPad.

### Where are my scans?

Scans are stored in Scanumi's local app container. Open a folder or Recent Scans to find them. Use **Share PDF** when you want to save or send a copy elsewhere. Apple device backups may include app data according to your settings.

### How do I remove a scan?

Open the scan, choose the More menu, and select **Delete Scan**. This removes its pages and generated PDF from Scanumi's local storage.

### Does Scanumi upload my documents?

Scanumi does not operate an account or document cloud service and does not upload scans to the developer. Copies can leave the app through Apple's share sheet, and Apple device backups may include app data according to your settings.
